Cash Receipt Recording Procedure

  1. Receive transaction evidence (e.g., receipt).
  2. Verify completeness and validity of transaction evidence.
  3. Identify involved accounts (e.g., Cash, Accounts Receivable/Revenue).
  4. Record transaction in the Cash Receipts Journal.
  5. Post to the General Ledger.
  6. Archive transaction evidence.
